While You Are Here

Explore Agra

Between the celebrations, take a little time to explore the timeless charm of Agra, a city where history, love and heritage come together beautifully. Wander through magnificent monuments, peaceful gardens and remarkable architectural treasures, each carrying a story of its own. From iconic landmarks to hidden moments of serenity, let the city add a memorable chapter to your journey. Whether you are here for a few hours or a little longer, we hope you enjoy discovering the beauty and spirit of Agra.

01

Taj Mahal Agra

A timeless symbol of love and one of India's most treasured landmarks.

02

Agra Fort

Explore magnificent architecture and centuries of Mughal history.

03

Mehtab Bagh

A beautiful garden retreat offering a serene view across the Yamuna.

04

Fatehpur Sikri

Discover the grand historic city and its remarkable architectural heritage.

05

Akbar's Tomb

A striking monument surrounded by peaceful gardens and historic charm.
