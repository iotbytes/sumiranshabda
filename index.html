While You Are Here

Explore Agra

Between the celebrations, take a little time to explore the timeless charm of Agra, a city where history, love and heritage come together beautifully. Wander through magnificent monuments, peaceful gardens and remarkable architectural treasures, each carrying a story of its own. From iconic landmarks to hidden moments of serenity, let the city add a memorable chapter to your journey. Whether you are here for a few hours or a little longer, we hope you enjoy discovering the beauty and spirit of Agra.

01

A timeless symbol of love and one of India's most treasured landmarks.

02

Agra Fort

03

Mehtab Bagh

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers before you arrive, because every great celebration needs a little planning.

How do I RSVP?

We would love to celebrate with you. Simply fill in the RSVP form below with your attendance and guest details, and let us know if you have a little message for us. ♡

How do I get to Dayalbagh, Agra?

Dayalbagh is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. Detailed directions and travel information will be shared with you to help make your journey smooth. The exact venue address will be shared soon. 📍

What should I wear?

No official dress code has been provided. We suggest festive Indian attire something elegant, comfortable and ready for a few photographs. Because someone has to look fabulous in the group photo! 📸

Will accommodation details be available?

Accommodation details will be shared soon. Travel information will also be updated soon, so there is no need to start packing just yet. 😉

Can I take lots of photographs?

Absolutely! Capture the smiles, the colours and the little moments. Just remember to put the phone down once in a while and enjoy the celebration with us too. 📸♡

Is there anything I should bring?

Just bring yourself, your blessings and your best festive spirit. Everything else can wait until the details are shared! ♡

What if I have never been to Agra before?

Welcome to your first Agra adventure! Keep the location saved, check the directions before you leave and let the map do the serious work. 😄📍

What if I arrive late?

Don't panic! Please check the confirmed event timings before heading over. For events where the timing is still being finalised, details will be shared soon. We would rather have you arrive a little late than miss the celebration entirely. ♡

What is the weather like in November in Agra?

November is usually pleasant in Agra, with comfortable days and cooler mornings and evenings. We recommend festive attire with a light layer for later in the day because looking elegant and staying comfortable should go hand in hand! ♡
